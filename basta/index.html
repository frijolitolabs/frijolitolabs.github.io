Basta: Stop & Tutti Frutti

The categories game for family night. The phone draws the letter, runs the clock and keeps score; you play on paper. No ads. · Español

Support

Questions, a bug, or a category pack you'd like to see? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Replies usually come within two days.

How do I play?
Everyone needs paper and a pen and writes the categories across the top of the page. The phone draws a letter; everyone writes one answer per category starting with that letter. Whoever finishes first taps ¡BASTA! and everyone stops (with 10 more seconds to finish a word, if that's on). Then, one category at a time, everyone reads their answer aloud: a unique answer scores 100, an answer someone else also wrote scores 50, no answer scores 0. Setup → How to play has the details.
Who decides whether an answer counts?
The table, the way Basta is really played. Tap the raised hand next to a player and everyone votes; a tie keeps the answer. There's no dictionary deciding for you.
Can I change the letters, the clock or the number of rounds?
Yes, in Setup: a 1 to 3 minute clock or no clock, 3, 5 or 8 rounds or "until we stop", and hard letters (K, Q, W, X, Y, Z, and Ñ in Spanish) on or off. Before the clock starts you can tap "New letter".
What's free and what does the purchase add?
The whole game is free with the Classic pack (name, country, animal, color, food and more). Basta Pro, a one-time purchase, adds five more category packs, your own categories, and scoring variants (10/5/0, and double points for the only answer in a category). No subscription, no ads.
I bought Pro on another device.
Setup → Basta Pro → Restore purchase. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account, and Family Sharing works.
The app closed in the middle of a game.
Open it again: the game in progress, scores included, is saved and picks up where it was.

Privacy policy

Basta collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Player names, your categories, your settings and the game in progress are stored only on your device.
  • The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Basta receives only confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees payment details.
  • Basta makes no network requests of its own.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.

Soporte

¿Preguntas, un error o un paquete de categorías que te gustaría? Escribe a frijolitolabs@gmail.com.

¿Cómo se juega?
Cada quien necesita una hoja y una pluma, y escribe las categorías arriba de la hoja. El teléfono saca una letra y todos escriben una respuesta por categoría que empiece con esa letra. Quien termine primero toca ¡BASTA! y todos dejan de escribir. Después, categoría por categoría, cada quien lee su respuesta: única vale 100, repetida 50, sin respuesta 0.
¿Quién decide si una respuesta cuenta?
La mesa. Toquen la mano levantada junto a un jugador y todos votan; si hay empate, la respuesta se queda.
¿Qué es gratis?
El juego completo es gratis con el paquete Clásico. Basta Pro, un solo pago, agrega cinco paquetes más, tus propias categorías y variantes de puntos. Sin suscripción, sin anuncios.

Aviso de privacidad

Basta no recopila ningún dato. No hay cuenta, analítica, publicidad ni SDK de terceros. Los nombres, tus categorías, tus ajustes y el juego en curso se guardan solo en tu dispositivo. La compra la maneja Apple; la app no hace conexiones a internet propias.
