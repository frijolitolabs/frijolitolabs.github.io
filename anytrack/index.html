Support

Questions, bugs or a tracker that doesn’t fit? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Include the app version from Settings → Help. Replies usually within two days.

Common questions

Where can I log from?
The app, Home Screen widgets, a Control Center button, and the Apple Watch app and complications. A log from any of them shows up everywhere.
What does Pro add?
Unlimited trackers (free includes 3 active ones), Reports with every tracker side by side, four extra themes and four alternate app icons. It’s a one-time purchase. Logging, history, charts, export, import and backups are never behind Pro.
How do I keep my data safe?
Anytrack saves a backup on your device once a day and keeps the last 14; Settings → Automatic backups restores any of them. You can also export everything as JSON (or entries as CSV) and import a backup on another device.
I bought Pro on another device.
Open Anytrack Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Pro is tied to your Apple Account and supports Family Sharing.
Does it sync between my iPhones?
Not yet. Your iPhone and Apple Watch sync with each other directly; to move to a new phone, export a backup and import it.

Privacy policy

Anytrack collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK.

  • Your trackers and entries are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, which sync with each other directly.
  • Data leaves your devices only when you export it yourself.
  • The Pro purchase is handled by Apple’s App Store; the app never sees payment details.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.
