Mentiroso: Liar's Dice

The bluffing dice game, the way it's played at the table. No ads, no coins, fair dice. · Español

Support

Questions, a bug, or house rules the app should support? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Replies usually come within two days.

How do I play?
Everyone has five dice under a cup and sees only their own. Take turns bidding on how many dice on the whole table show a face ("four 5s"); each bid must go up. Think the last bid is too high? Call Liar! and everyone's dice are counted: if the bid holds you lose a die, if not the bidder does. The last player with dice wins. Setup → How to play has the full rules.
What do "ones are wild" and "calzo" mean?
Wild ones count as every face. You can't open with ones; switching to ones needs half the number, switching back needs double plus one. Calzo is calling the bid exactly right: if it is, you win a die back; if not, you lose one.
Can we play with several people on one phone?
Yes. Set more than one player to "person" in Setup. Before each person's turn the app shows "Pass the phone to …" with nothing secret on screen, and that person taps to see their own dice.
How do I know the dice are fair?
Each round's dice come from a random seed. A fingerprint of the seed is shown while you bid, and the seed itself when the cups lift, so any roll can be checked. The dice can't change once the fingerprint is shown.
What's free and what does the purchase add?
The whole game is free for up to 4 players with the standard rules. Mentiroso Pro, a one-time purchase, adds up to 6 players and house rules (ones not wild, no calzo). No subscription, no ads.
I bought Pro on another device.
Setup → Mentiroso Pro → Restore purchase. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account.
The app closed in the middle of a game.
Open it again: the game in progress is saved and picks up where it was.

Privacy policy

Mentiroso collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Player names, your settings and the game in progress are stored only on your device.
  • The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Mentiroso receives only confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees payment details.
  • Mentiroso makes no network requests of its own.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.

Soporte

¿Preguntas, un error o reglas de tu casa que la app debería tener? Escribe a frijolitolabs@gmail.com.

¿Cómo se juega?
Cada quien tiene cinco dados bajo un cubilete y solo ve los suyos. Por turnos, apuesten cuántos dados de toda la mesa muestran una cara ("cuatro 5"); cada apuesta debe subir. ¿Crees que la última es muy alta? Grita ¡Mentiroso! y se cuentan los dados: si la apuesta se cumple pierdes un dado; si no, lo pierde quien apostó. Gana el último jugador con dados.
¿Podemos jugar varias personas en un teléfono?
Sí. En la configuración marca a más de un jugador como "persona". Antes de cada turno la app pide pasar el teléfono y cada quien toca para ver solo sus dados.
¿Qué es gratis?
El juego completo es gratis hasta para 4 jugadores con las reglas normales. Mentiroso Pro, un solo pago, agrega hasta 6 jugadores y reglas de la casa. Sin suscripción, sin anuncios.

Aviso de privacidad

Mentiroso no recopila ningún dato. No hay cuenta, analítica, publicidad ni SDK de terceros. Los nombres, tus ajustes y el juego en curso se guardan solo en tu dispositivo. La compra la maneja Apple; la app no hace conexiones a internet propias.
