Pirinola: Toma Todo

The pirinola for family parties: spin, toma todo, and the app counts the beans. Fair spins, no ads. · Español

Support

Questions, a bug, or a house rule you'd like? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Replies usually come within two days.

How do I play?
Everyone starts with 10 beans and puts 1 in the pot. Take turns spinning and do what the top says: Pon 1 or Pon 2 (put in), Toma 1 or Toma 2 (take out), Toma todo (take the whole pot) or Todos ponen (everyone puts 1). When the pot is empty, everyone antes again. Run out of beans and you're out. The last player with beans wins, or when the spins run out, the most beans wins.
We have real beans. Can we just use the spinner?
Yes: choose Real beans in Setup. The app only spins; your table counts the beans or candy. It's free.
How do I spin?
Tap the top, swipe it, or shake the phone.
How do I know the spins are fair?
Every spin comes from a random seed. A fingerprint of the seed is shown from the start of the game and the seed itself at the end, so anyone can re-derive every spin.
What's free and what does the purchase add?
The whole game is free for up to 4 players with the standard rules, and Real beans mode is free. Pirinola Pro, a one-time purchase, adds up to 10 players and house rules (5 to 50 starting beans, ante 1 to 5).
I bought Pro on another device.
Setup → Pirinola Pro → Restore purchase. Family Sharing works.

Privacy policy

Pirinola collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Player names, your settings and the game in progress are stored only on your device.
  • The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Pirinola receives only confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees payment details.
  • Pirinola makes no network requests of its own.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.

Soporte

¿Preguntas, un error o una regla de la casa que te gustaría? Escribe a frijolitolabs@gmail.com.

¿Cómo se juega?
Cada quien empieza con 10 frijoles y pone 1 en el bote. Por turnos, giren la pirinola y hagan lo que diga: Pon 1, Pon 2, Toma 1, Toma 2, Toma todo o Todos ponen. Cuando el bote se vacía, todos vuelven a poner. Si te quedas sin frijoles, quedas fuera. Gana el último con frijoles o, al acabarse los giros, quien tenga más.
¿Podemos usar frijoles de verdad?
Sí: elige "Frijoles de verdad" y la app solo gira. Es gratis.
¿Qué es gratis?
El juego completo es gratis hasta para 4 jugadores. Pirinola Pro, un solo pago, agrega hasta 10 jugadores y reglas de la casa. Sin suscripción, sin anuncios.

Aviso de privacidad

Pirinola no recopila ningún dato. No hay cuenta, analítica, publicidad ni SDK de terceros. Los nombres, tus ajustes y el juego en curso se guardan solo en tu dispositivo. La compra la maneja Apple; la app no hace conexiones a internet propias.
