How do I play?
Everyone starts with 10 beans and puts 1 in the pot. Take turns spinning and do what the top says: Pon 1 or Pon 2 (put in), Toma 1 or Toma 2 (take out), Toma todo (take the whole pot) or Todos ponen (everyone puts 1). When the pot is empty, everyone puts beans in again. Run out of beans and you're out. The last player with beans wins, or when the spins run out, the most beans wins.
We have real beans. Can we just use the spinner?
Yes: choose Real beans in Setup. The app only spins; your table counts the beans or candy. It's free.
How do I spin?
Tap the top, swipe it, or shake the phone.
How do I know the spins are fair?
Every spin comes from a random seed. A fingerprint of the seed is shown from the start of the game and the seed itself at the end, so anyone can re-derive every spin.
What's free and what does the purchase add?
The whole game is free for up to 4 players with the standard rules, and Real beans mode is free. Pirinola Pro, a one-time purchase, adds up to 10 players and house rules (5 to 50 starting beans, 1 to 5 beans per pot).

How to check the spins

Before the first spin, Pirinola picks a random 32-byte seed and shows the start of its fingerprint (the SHA-256 of the seed). Every spin is fixed by that seed, so nothing can change once the fingerprint is on screen. At the end the app shows the whole seed and fingerprint (in Real beans, tap Show seed and start a new one). Tap Copy, then:

  1. Check that SHA-256 of the seed equals the fingerprint.
  2. Spin i (counting from 0) = the first 8 bytes of SHA-256(seed followed by i as 4 big-endian bytes), read as a number, mod 6, in this order: Pon 1, Pon 2, Toma 1, Toma 2, Toma todo, Todos ponen.
import hashlib
seed = bytes.fromhex("PASTE THE SEED HERE")
print(hashlib.sha256(seed).hexdigest())  # must match the fingerprint
faces = ["Pon 1", "Pon 2", "Toma 1", "Toma 2", "Toma todo", "Todos ponen"]
for i in range(50):
    h = hashlib.sha256(seed + i.to_bytes(4, "big")).digest()
    print(i + 1, faces[int.from_bytes(h[:8], "big") % 6])

Cómo verificar los giros

Antes del primer giro, Pirinola elige una semilla aleatoria de 32 bytes y muestra el inicio de su huella (el SHA-256 de la semilla). Cada giro queda fijado por esa semilla. Al final la app muestra la semilla y la huella completas (en Frijoles de verdad, toca Mostrar semilla y empezar otra). Toca Copiar y luego: revisa que el SHA-256 de la semilla sea la huella; el giro i (desde 0) son los primeros 8 bytes de SHA-256(semilla seguida de i en 4 bytes big-endian), como número, mod 6, en este orden: Pon 1, Pon 2, Toma 1, Toma 2, Toma todo, Todos ponen. El programa de arriba (Python) lo hace por ti.
