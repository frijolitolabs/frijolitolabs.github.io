Support

Questions, bugs, or a rule your table plays that Bower doesn't? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Include the app version from Settings → Help and, for a rules question, the hand review's seed if you have it. Replies usually within two days.

Common questions

Does the AI see my cards?
No. The AI is a separate module that only receives what a player in its seat would know: its own hand, the up card, bids, and cards played. Difficulty changes how much it searches, never what it can see.
How do I know the deal wasn't changed mid-hand?
Each hand's deck comes from a random seed. Before the deal, the app shows a SHA-256 commitment of that seed; after the hand, it reveals the seed. The review screen re-derives the deal and shows a check mark if it matches, and the derivation is documented so anyone can verify it independently.
What does Pro add?
Spades (nil, bags and a partner that bids sensibly), expert opponents, table themes and card backs, and stats export. It's a one-time purchase. The free euchre game is complete: every rule, every stat, verifiable deals, and no ads.
I bought Pro on another device.
Settings → Pro → Restore purchase. Pro is tied to your Apple Account and supports Family Sharing.
Which rules are supported?
Standard North American euchre with options for stick the dealer, Canadian loner, defend alone, and games to 5, 7, 10, 11 or 15.

Privacy policy

Bower collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Your games, settings and fairness statistics are stored only on your device. They leave it only if you export them yourself (for example, sharing the stats CSV).
  • The Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Bower receives only confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees payment details.
  • Bower makes no network requests of its own.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.
