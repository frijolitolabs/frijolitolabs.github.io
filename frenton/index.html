Frentón

Family charades in English, Spanish or both. No ads, pay once. · Español

Support

Questions, a bug, or a word that should be in a deck? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Include the app version from Settings. Replies usually come within two days.

How do I play?
Hold the phone sideways against your forehead, screen facing everyone else. They act out or hint at the word. Tilt the phone down when you guess it, up to pass. When time runs out, pass the phone on.
Tilting doesn't register.
Start each turn with the phone upright; a tilt counts once and the phone has to come back upright before the next one. If tilting isn't comfortable, Settings → Tap instead of tilt.
What's free and what does the purchase add?
Navidad, Animales and Kids are free and complete. Frentón Completo, a one-time purchase, unlocks the other nine decks, teams and your own decks. No subscription, no ads, no deck store.
I bought it on another device.
Settings → Restore purchase. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account and supports Family Sharing.

Privacy policy

Frentón collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Your settings and the decks you write are stored only on your device.
  • The motion sensor is used only during a turn, on your device, to detect a tilt; nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Record the round is off unless you turn it on. When it's on, Frentón asks for the camera and microphone and films each turn with the front camera so you can watch it back. The video is kept only on your device and is deleted when you start the next turn or end the game. It leaves the phone only if you tap Share yourself.
  • The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Frentón receives only confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees payment details.
  • Frentón makes no network requests of its own.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date below.

Soporte

¿Preguntas, un error o una palabra que debería estar en una baraja? Escribe a frijolitolabs@gmail.com e incluye la versión de la app (Ajustes).

¿Cómo se juega?
Pon el celular de lado en tu frente, con la pantalla hacia los demás. Ellos actúan o dan pistas. Inclínalo hacia abajo cuando adivines y hacia arriba para pasar. Al terminar el tiempo, pasa el celular.
¿Qué es gratis?
Navidad, Animales y Niños son gratis y completas. Frentón Completo, una sola compra, desbloquea las otras nueve barajas, los equipos y tus propias barajas. Sin suscripción, sin anuncios.
Ya la compré en otro dispositivo.
Ajustes → Restaurar compra. Incluye En Familia.

Aviso de privacidad

Frentón no recopila ningún dato. No hay cuenta, analítica, publicidad ni SDK de terceros. Tus ajustes y barajas se guardan solo en tu dispositivo; el sensor de movimiento se usa solo en tu dispositivo durante el turno; la compra la maneja Apple; la app no hace conexiones a internet.

Grabar la ronda está apagado a menos que lo actives. Si lo activas, Frentón pide la cámara y el micrófono y graba cada turno con la cámara frontal para que lo puedan volver a ver. El video se guarda solo en tu dispositivo y se borra al empezar el siguiente turno o terminar el juego. Solo sale del celular si tú lo compartes.
