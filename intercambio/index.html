Intercambio

Run the white elephant gift exchange at your party from one phone. No ads, pay once. · Español

Support

Questions, a bug, or house rules the app should support? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Replies usually come within two days.

How does it work?
Add the players and pick a game (White Elephant, Yankee Swap or Dirty Santa / Robo). The app shuffles the turn order. On each turn the player opens a new gift or steals one that's already open; whoever was robbed goes next. Gifts lock after the steal limit, and nobody can steal straight back the gift just taken from them. The first player gets a final swap at the end.
Someone tapped the wrong thing.
Undo (top left) reverses the last action, as many times as you need.
The app closed in the middle of the party.
Open it again: the game is saved after every action and picks up where it was.
What's free and what does the purchase add?
The whole game is free for up to 12 players. Intercambio Pro, a one-time purchase, adds up to 60 players, saved groups, gift photos and showing the game on a TV. No subscription, no ads.
I bought it on another device.
Setup → Intercambio Pro → Restore purchase. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account and supports Family Sharing.

Privacy policy

Intercambio collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Player names, saved groups and the game in progress are stored only on your device.
  • Gift photos (Pro) are taken with the camera or chosen from your library only when you ask. They are kept only on your device and are deleted when the party ends.
  • The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Intercambio receives only confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees payment details.
  • Intercambio makes no network requests of its own.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date below.

Soporte

¿Preguntas, un error o reglas de tu casa que la app debería tener? Escribe a frijolitolabs@gmail.com.

¿Cómo funciona?
Agrega a los jugadores y escoge el juego (Elefante Blanco, Yankee Swap o Robo). La app revuelve el orden de turnos. En cada turno se abre un regalo nuevo o se roba uno ya abierto; le toca a quien robaron. Los regalos se ponen candado al llegar al límite de robos y no se vale robarlo de regreso. Quien abrió primero tiene un último cambio.
¿Qué es gratis?
El juego completo es gratis hasta 12 jugadores. Intercambio Pro, un solo pago, agrega hasta 60 jugadores, grupos guardados, fotos de los regalos y el juego en la tele. Sin suscripción, sin anuncios.
Ya la compré en otro dispositivo.
Intercambio Pro → Restaurar compra. Incluye En Familia.

Aviso de privacidad

Intercambio no recopila ningún dato. No hay cuenta, analítica, publicidad ni SDK de terceros. Los nombres, grupos y el juego se guardan solo en tu dispositivo. Las fotos de los regalos (Pro) se toman solo cuando tú lo pides, se quedan en tu dispositivo y se borran al terminar el intercambio. La compra la maneja Apple; la app no hace conexiones a internet.
