Which rules can I play?
Mexican (game to 100; the team that goes out scores the other team's pips), Puerto Rican (game to 200, with capicú and chuchazo bonuses) and All Fives (score whenever the open ends add to a multiple of five; game to 250; the first double is a spinner: once both its sides have a tile, its top and bottom open too, so up to four ends count). Pick one on the home screen or in Settings.
How do I play a tile?
Tap it. If it fits more than one end, tap the end you want. If you have nothing to play, the app passes for you and shows it on the table.
What happens when nobody can play?
That's a tranque: the hand ends and the side with the fewest pips wins it. The hand summary shows everyone's leftover tiles.
How do I play online with family?
Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then tap "Play with family" and invite them. Each person plays on their own phone and takes turns at their own pace.
How do I know the deal is fair?
Every hand is dealt from a random seed whose fingerprint is shown during play; the seed itself is shown when the hand ends, so anyone can check the deal. See How to check the deal.

How to check the deal

Before each hand, the app picks a random 32-byte seed and shows the start of its fingerprint (the SHA-256 of the seed) at the top of the table. Every player's seven tiles come from that seed, so nothing can change once the fingerprint is on screen. When the hand ends, the app shows the whole seed and fingerprint. Tap Copy, then:

  1. Check that SHA-256 of the seed equals the fingerprint.
  2. Start from the double-six set in order 0-0, 0-1, … 0-6, 1-1, … 6-6 (28 tiles) and shuffle it (Fisher–Yates): for i from 27 down to 1, take the first 8 bytes of SHA-256(seed followed by i as 4 big-endian bytes) as a number r, and swap tiles i and r mod (i + 1).
  3. Deal in seat order, seven tiles each: the first seat gets tiles 0–6 of the shuffled set, the next seat 7–13, then 14–20 and 21–27. Nothing is left over. Against the computer the seats are you, Rosa, Beto and Memo, in playing order (Beto is your partner). In the first hand of a game the 6-6 leads.
import hashlib
seed = bytes.fromhex("PASTE THE SEED HERE")
print(hashlib.sha256(seed).hexdigest())  # must match the fingerprint
tiles = [(a, b) for a in range(7) for b in range(a, 7)]  # 0-0, 0-1, ... 0-6, 1-1, ... 6-6
for i in range(len(tiles) - 1, 0, -1):
    h = hashlib.sha256(seed + i.to_bytes(4, "big")).digest()
    j = int.from_bytes(h[:8], "big") % (i + 1)
    tiles[i], tiles[j] = tiles[j], tiles[i]
names = ["You", "Rosa", "Beto", "Memo"]  # seats in playing order, against the computer
for s in range(4):
    print(names[s], sorted(tiles[s * 7:(s + 1) * 7]))

Privacy policy

Dominó en Familia collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Your settings (rules, game speed, computer level) and a game in progress against the computer are stored only on your device.
  • Online games use Apple's Game Center. Your Game Center name and the moves of a shared game go through Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app's developer never receives them.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own and has nothing to buy.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date below.

¿Qué reglas puedo jugar?
Mexicano (partida a 100; el equipo que gana cuenta los puntos del otro), Puertorriqueño (partida a 200, con bonos de capicúa y chuchazo) y Cincos (anotas cuando las puntas suman múltiplo de cinco; partida a 250; la primera mula es la cruz: cuando tiene fichas a sus dos lados, se abre también arriba y abajo, y cuentan hasta cuatro puntas).
¿Cómo juego una ficha?
Tócala. Si entra por más de una punta, toca la punta que quieras. Si no llevas, la app pasa por ti.
¿Cómo juego en línea con la familia?
Inicia sesión en Game Center en la app Ajustes, toca "Jugar con la familia" e invítalos. Cada quien juega en su teléfono, a su ritmo.
¿Cómo sé que el reparto es justo?
Cada mano sale de una semilla aleatoria cuya huella se ve durante el juego; al terminar la mano se muestra la semilla, para que cualquiera pueda comprobar el reparto. Mira Cómo verificar el reparto.

Cómo verificar el reparto

Antes de cada mano, la app elige una semilla aleatoria de 32 bytes y muestra arriba de la mesa el inicio de su huella (el SHA-256 de la semilla). Las siete fichas de cada jugador salen de esa semilla. Al terminar la mano, la app muestra la semilla y la huella completas. Toca Copiar y luego: revisa que el SHA-256 de la semilla sea la huella; baraja el juego doble seis en orden 0-0, 0-1, … 0-6, 1-1, … 6-6 (28 fichas) con Fisher–Yates (para i de 27 a 1, los primeros 8 bytes de SHA-256(semilla seguida de i en 4 bytes big-endian) como número r; intercambia i y r mod (i + 1)); reparte en orden de asientos siete fichas a cada quien: el primer asiento recibe las fichas 0–6 de la baraja, el siguiente 7–13, luego 14–20 y 21–27. No sobra ninguna. Contra la computadora los asientos son tú, Rosa, Beto y Memo, en orden de juego (Beto es tu pareja). En la primera mano de la partida sale la mula de seis. El programa de arriba (Python) lo hace por ti.
