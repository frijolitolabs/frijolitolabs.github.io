Dominó en Familia

Four-player partner dominoes the way your family plays. No ads, no coins. · Español

Support

Questions, a bug, or house rules the app should support? Email frijolitolabs@gmail.com. Replies usually come within two days.

Which rules can I play?
Mexican (game to 100; the team that goes out scores the other team's pips), Puerto Rican (game to 200, with capicú and chuchazo bonuses) and All Fives (score whenever the open ends add to a multiple of five; game to 250). Pick one on the home screen or in Settings.
How do I play a tile?
Tap it. If it fits both ends, tap the end you want. If you have nothing to play, the app passes for you and shows it on the table.
What happens when nobody can play?
That's a tranque: the hand ends and the side with the fewest pips wins it. The hand summary shows everyone's leftover tiles.
How do I play online with family?
Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then tap "Play with family" and invite them. Each person plays on their own phone and takes turns at their own pace.
How do I know the deal is fair?
Every hand is shuffled on your phone from a random seed, and the deal's fingerprint is shown at the end of the hand so it can be checked.

Privacy policy

Dominó en Familia collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK.

  • Your settings (rules, game speed, computer level) are stored only on your device.
  • Online games use Apple's Game Center. Your Game Center name and the moves of a shared game go through Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app's developer never receives them.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own and has nothing to buy.
  • If you email support, your message is used only to answer you.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the date below.

Soporte

¿Preguntas, un error o reglas de tu casa que la app debería tener? Escribe a frijolitolabs@gmail.com.

¿Qué reglas puedo jugar?
Mexicano (partida a 100; el equipo que gana cuenta los puntos del otro), Puertorriqueño (partida a 200, con bonos de capicúa y chuchazo) y Cincos (anotas cuando las puntas suman múltiplo de cinco; partida a 250).
¿Cómo juego una ficha?
Tócala. Si entra por las dos puntas, toca la punta que quieras. Si no llevas, la app pasa por ti.
¿Cómo juego en línea con la familia?
Inicia sesión en Game Center en la app Ajustes, toca "Jugar con la familia" e invítalos. Cada quien juega en su teléfono, a su ritmo.

Aviso de privacidad

Dominó en Familia no recopila ningún dato. No hay cuenta, analítica, publicidad ni SDK de terceros. Tus ajustes se guardan solo en tu dispositivo. Las partidas en línea pasan por Game Center de Apple; la app no hace conexiones a internet propias.
